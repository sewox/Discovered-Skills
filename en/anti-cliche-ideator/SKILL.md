# Anti-Cliche Ideator (Exclusionary Baseline Ideation)

A divergent ideation engine that treats predictable, generic AI outputs as a "negative boundary" (a direction NOT to take), systematically exploring unconventional, high-signal ideas strictly outside that mediocrity baseline.

## When to Use

- When brainstorming marketing campaigns, product features, strategic initiatives, or creative content where generic tropes must be avoided
- When a user asks to break through creative ruts and skip "first-order obvious answers"
- When given instructions like "don't give me generic ideas, avoid what everyone else does"
- When running structured ideation sessions using negative constraints (anti-briefs)

## 4-Stage Operational Protocol

### 1. Stage 1: The Baseline Sweep (Ezber Taraması)
Catalog the top 5-10 obvious, superficial ideas, concepts, or tropes that an average LLM or typical competitor would generate for the given prompt.
- Explicitly label this list as the "Mediocrity Baseline".

### 2. Stage 2: Negative Constraint Mapping (Yasaklı Bölge)
Declare every theme, metaphor, and vector identified in Stage 1 strictly forbidden:
- "None of these concepts, patterns, or obvious tropes may be used."
- Establish the perimeter of ideas to avoid.

### 3. Stage 3: Orthogonal Divergence (Sınır Dışı Arama)
Force ideation outside the prohibited boundary using proven creative divergence levers:
- **Inversion:** What if the exact opposite of the conventional premise were true?
- **Cross-Domain Grafting:** Borrow mental models from distant fields (e.g. mycology, supply chain logistics, aviation).
- **Extreme Constraints:** What if the budget were zero, latency had to be sub-millisecond, or the primary medium were banned?
- Synthesize 3-5 deeply differentiated, compelling concepts.

### 4. Stage 4: Separation Audit (Ayrışma Denetimi)
Stress-test each newly generated concept against the Stage 1 baseline:
- "Does this concept share any DNA with the obvious tropes?"
- Prune or re-angle anything that edges close to the mediocrity baseline.

## Output Format

1. **Forbidden Baseline (Anti-Brief):** The 5 obvious ideas everyone else would propose.
2. **Divergent Concepts:** 3 breakthrough alternatives strictly outside the boundary (Each containing: *Strategic Angle*, *Core Mechanism*, *Why It Breaks the Cliché*).
3. **Separation Audit:** A brief justification detailing how the proposals structurally diverge from the baseline.

## Gotchas

- Do not generate impractical absurdities just to be different; balance novelty with operational feasibility.
- Do not merely reskin a Stage 1 cliché with new buzzwords; the underlying mechanism must be genuinely orthogonal.
